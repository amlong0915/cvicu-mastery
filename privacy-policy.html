CVICU: Mastering The Bedside — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

CVICU: Mastering The Bedside ("we", "our", "the App") is an educational study application for critical care and cardiac nursing professionals.

1. Data We Collect

  • Local Study Progress: Quiz answers, bookmarks, spaced-repetition intervals, and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases: Subscriptions are processed securely by Apple App Store and Google Play via RevenueCat using an anonymous app user ID. We do not collect or store credit card numbers.
  • No Patient Health Information (PHI): The App does not collect, store, or transmit Protected Health Information. Do not enter patient-identifying data into any calculator or form.
